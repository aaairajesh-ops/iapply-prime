STEP 1

Switch it on

  1. Log in to your partner dashboard at iapply.io/outreachs/login.
  2. Open the Liveclass item in the left menu.
  3. Press Activate my academy. That is the whole step.

Nothing to install, nothing to pay. If the menu item is missing, your account manager can enable it on a call.
